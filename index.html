The answers

How to use it

  1. Photograph each page. Press the big button and take one photo per page, in the order they are printed. Flat on a table, in good light — the answers are only as good as the small print.
  2. Or pick them out of your gallery. The button on the left opens it, and the number on it is how many pages you have so far. Tap a page to take it out or move it.
  3. Press ✓ when the paper is complete. Every question printed on the pages comes back answered — multiple choice, fill in the blank, open-ended and lettered parts alike.

A question that carries on over the page is fine: the pages are read as one paper, so it is stitched back together instead of coming back as two halves.

It marks what is already written

You do not have to choose. Every question is looked at for an answer already written on the paper: where there is one it is marked — right, partly right or wrong — with a line or two of feedback saying exactly where it went wrong and what to do instead, and the correct answer beside it. Where the question is still blank it is simply answered. A page holding some of each comes back with some of each.

The correct answer is always worked out from scratch first, so what the student wrote can never talk the app into the wrong answer.

Four subjects

Science, Mathematics, English and Chinese. A Chinese paper is answered and marked in Chinese; an English comprehension gets an answer in full sentences, and a maths question gets the working line by line.

Nothing is saved anywhere. Your paper lives in this tab and leaves through Copy or Print.
